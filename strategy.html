Core Strategy • मुख्य रणनीति

जागरूकता से लेकर कार्यवाही तक, समर्थन से लेकर आत्मनिर्भरता तक।

The strategy page is now focused only on how the foundation's documented activities connect with one another.

Strategic Pillars / रणनीतिक स्तंभ

1. Education First

Use literacy drives, learning-material support, household outreach and student activities to improve educational participation. The reports specifically record distribution of school bags, dresses, notebooks, books, pens and geometry boxes, along with motivation for parents and children.

2. Health & Prevention

Combine medical camps, child health activities, nutrition awareness, vaccination information, yoga awareness and tobacco-risk communication. The goal is to take basic awareness closer to communities while encouraging timely use of available services.

3. Livelihood & Skills

Training in fruit preservation, carpentry, horticulture and dress making demonstrates a practical route from skill acquisition to self-employment. Post-training guidance and tools strengthen the livelihood pathway.

4. Women & Children

Prioritise maternal and child wellbeing, nutrition, vaccination, safety, government-scheme awareness and girls' education. The strategy treats family awareness as an important part of long-term development.

5. Dignity-Based Welfare

Food, blankets, slippers and hospital fruit distribution address immediate needs. The strategy is to combine material support with respectful interaction, awareness and community participation.

6. Responsible Communities

Road safety, anti-tobacco, de-addiction, environmental protection, disability welfare and social-justice programmes address behaviours and systems that influence community wellbeing.

Implementation Method / कार्यान्वयन पद्धति

  1. Identify a local issue through community interaction or programme needs.
  2. Choose an appropriate awareness, service, training or support activity.
  3. Involve local schools, volunteers, community members and relevant stakeholders where appropriate.
  4. Conduct the activity with a clear beneficiary focus.
  5. Record the programme through reports, photographs and supporting documents.
  6. Review outcomes and identify areas for continuity.

This approach is consistent with the practical character of the annual reports: the organisation documents both awareness activities and direct interventions, rather than treating communication and service delivery as separate worlds.

Why this model matters / यह मॉडल क्यों जरूरी है

Rural development issues overlap. A child who lacks learning support may also live in a family facing livelihood constraints. A woman who needs health information may also need awareness of government schemes. A vulnerable person receiving relief may also benefit from sanitation, road-safety or health awareness. A young person receiving vocational training needs both a practical skill and guidance on using that skill.

For this reason, the foundation's multi-sector approach is useful: it allows different programmes to reinforce one another while keeping community needs at the centre.

Strategy in the 2023–24, 2024–25 & 2025–26 Reports

The three reports show continuity rather than a single isolated programme. The 2023–24 record includes literacy, vocational training, environmental work, child welfare, disability welfare, population-control and health awareness, road safety, food and blanket distribution, medical camps, agriculture awareness and commemorative programmes. The 2024–25 record adds or continues women and child development, cultural programmes, yoga, tobacco-free awareness, hospital fruit distribution, de-addiction awareness, sports, creative competitions and slippers distribution. The 2025–26 record continues this pattern through vocational and soft-skill training, environmental plantation drives, women and child development guidance, a cultural programme, International Yoga Day, Tobacco-Free Day participation and food distribution.

The strategy therefore rests on four linked ideas: reach people where they are, create awareness, provide practical support, and document the work.
